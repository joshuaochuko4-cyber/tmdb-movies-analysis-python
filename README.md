# tmdb-movies-analysis-python
Performed EDA on the TMDB Movies dataset using Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn). Analyzed revenue vs budget correlation, top-grossing films, genre trends, and profitability. Produced 4 visualizations including a correlation heatmap and popularity rankings. Tools: Python · Pandas · Seaborn
# 🎬 TMDB Movies Data Analysis

### Built with Python | By Isah Joshua

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA) on the TMDB Movies dataset 
using Python to uncover insights around revenue performance, movie 
popularity, genre trends, and the relationship between budget and 
revenue.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
The film industry generates massive amounts of data that is difficult 
to interpret without structured analysis. This project explores what 
factors drive movie revenue, popularity, and audience ratings.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze the TMDB movies dataset and extract meaningful insights 
around financial performance, audience behavior, and production 
trends using Python.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Identified the highest-grossing movie and its director
- Calculated average budget and average revenue across all movies
- Found the most frequent genre in the dataset
- Determined the most voted and highest rated movies
- Identified the director with the most films
- Calculated percentage of movies that made a profit
- Analyzed average popularity per genre (Top 5)
- Explored movies released across different years

---

## 📊 Visualizations
- **Revenue vs Budget** — scatter plot showing correlation between 
  production spend and earnings
- **Top 10 Most Popular Movies** — horizontal bar chart ranked by 
  popularity score
- **Movies Released per Year** — line chart tracking production 
  trends over time
- **Correlation Heatmap** — showing relationships between budget, 
  revenue, popularity, vote average, and vote count

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- A strong positive correlation exists between budget and revenue
- A small number of movies account for the majority of total revenue
- Most movies in the dataset made a profit
- Popularity scores are heavily skewed toward a few blockbuster titles
- Movie production volume has grown significantly over the years

---

## 💼 Business Impact
This analysis helps studios, investors, and producers understand 
what drives box office success — enabling smarter budget allocation, 
genre selection, and release planning decisions.
